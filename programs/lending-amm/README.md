# Lending AMM

The lending AMM, published under the name in [`metadata.json`](metadata.json) and in the program's embedded `security.txt`, the only two places it lives ([SUITE-20](../../docs/decisions.md#suite-20-names-of-the-on-chain-products)), is a strategy for [Pipeline](https://github.com/MetalLegBob/pipeline)'s router: each market pairs a token with a lent mint, WSOL or USDC, on a constant-product curve with open LP shares.
Most of the lent side is lent to one fixed Kamino Lend reserve through its cToken instructions, while a buffer stays in the market's own vault for swaps.
Prices come from the market's total reserves, the buffer plus the market's cTokens at the reserve's stored rate on the lent side.
Anyone may call `rebalance`, which lends or redeems to bring the buffer back to its target share, and which puts the market into withdraw mode while Kamino's free liquidity runs short.
A swap that pays out more than the buffer holds redeems the shortfall from Kamino inside the swap.
LPs remove in cash or in kind, so they can leave whatever state Kamino is in; the LP fee and all the lending yield go to them, and they bear Kamino's losses.
The program has no admin.

The full rules are in [`docs/spec/lending-pool.md`](../../docs/spec/lending-pool.md), and the terms (lending AMM market, buffer, withdraw mode, in kind) in [`GLOSSARY.md`](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## Using it

- **Metadata**: [`metadata.json`](metadata.json), Pipeline's `pipeline-strategy-metadata/1`, describes market creation (`init`), seeding from another program (`seed_liquidity`) and every custom action (`aviary_actions`), so a terminal can build each transaction with Pipeline's SDK.
- **IDL and client**: the Anchor IDL is [`idl/lending_amm.json`](idl/lending_amm.json), and [`scripts/client/`](scripts/client/) is a TypeScript client generated from it with Codama.
- **Demo**: [`scripts/demo.ts`](scripts/demo.ts) creates, funds, rebalances and trades a market built from the metadata, and takes it through withdraw mode and Kamino's emergency mode, on a local validator or on devnet; [`docs/toolchain.md`](../../docs/toolchain.md) says how to build, test, deploy and run it.
- **Security**: report a vulnerability through this repo's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/imDev2023/testing-pipeline/security/advisories/new).
  The program has had internal reviews only ([`docs/security/lending-amm/`](../../docs/security/lending-amm/)), and no external audit.
