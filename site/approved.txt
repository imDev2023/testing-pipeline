**What `pool4.json` (`4ajWF6Y3...`) is:** MetalLegBob's own Pool4 port, not a four-mint pool. The census label in `research/pipeline-router.md` is wrong.
Each market has 2 mints (a Token-2022 token and wSOL), trades exact-in only, and is created when a token graduates from his bonding-curve strategy through `pipeline_seed_liquidity`.
The source is not public, the metadata is a placeholder, and he registered it on 2026-11-08. Several of these facts are inferred from replaying the market's trades; the file marks which.
Pipeline's `test-hook` approves everything when its extra accounts are left out, and Token-2022 does call hooks without them, so its allowlist can be skipped. Build on the Factory's fail-closed pattern instead: every account required, the `transferring` check, no CPI.

In the citations below, `pipeline:` means the Pipeline repo at `a2d5bfb`, `fff:` the Factory repo at `da2529b`, `t22:` Token-2022 at `486240c`, `phi:` the pinocchio hook interface 0.1.0, and `kb:` the Jupiter and Meteora knowledge base.
Pipeline's `test-hook` allowlist fails open when a caller leaves out its extra accounts, so the containment hook must not start from it; the Factory's hook shows the fail-closed pattern to copy.
The Factory's hook and Pipeline's `test-hook` both pass a transfer when its source or its destination is allowed (`fff:programs/transfer-hook/src/instructions/transfer_hook.rs:94-111`; `pipeline:programs/test-hook/src/lib.rs:42-58`). With the allowed set equal to {home market's H vault, market's H fee account}, every flow in the table passes:
Token-2022 calls the hook with only its four base accounts (source, mint, destination, authority) when the transfer does not include the hook's validation account; the validation account and every extra account are optional (`phi:src/instructions/execute.rs:139-179`; same in `spl-transfer-hook-interface` at `5949f23`, `interface/src/onchain.rs:15-55`). Pipeline's `test-hook` approves any transfer when its whitelist account is missing (`pipeline:programs/test-hook/src/lib.rs:45-47`), so anyone can skip its allowlist by sending a plain `transfer_checked` that names the hook program but leaves out the validation account. It is a test fixture, but `research/estimates.md` proposed starting from it; the containment hook must instead refuse every transfer unless the validation account and its config account are present and correct. The Factory's hook does this: its Anchor accounts struct requires all seven accounts with a seeds check on the validation account, it refuses zero amounts, checks the mint's owner, and checks Token-2022's `transferring` flag on the source so that nobody can call `Execute` directly (`fff:programs/transfer-hook/src/instructions/transfer_hook.rs:23-57`, `:79-156`). Token-2022 sets `transferring` on both accounts only for the duration of the hook call (`t22:program/src/processor.rs:611-636`; flag defined at `t22:interface/src/extension/transfer_hook/mod.rs:39-48`). For confidential transfers Token-2022 passes `u64::MAX` as the amount (`phi:src/instructions/execute.rs:63-67`), so a contained mint should not carry the confidential transfer extension, or the hook must refuse that amount.
Every hook call in a router sell (vault, fee account, integrator) gets the same tail of hook accounts (`pipeline:programs/pipeline/src/lib.rs:447-450`, `:472-571`; Pipeline's audit item H040, "One hook tail serves three transfers", `pipeline:docs/SECURITY-AUDIT.md:695`). Token-2022 resolves each extra account by key from the accounts it is given, so a superset works (`phi:src/instructions/execute.rs:229-247`). The Factory resolves one whitelist PDA per side, seeded by the token account (`fff:programs/transfer-hook/src/instructions/initialize_extra_account_meta_list.rs:28-54`), so a router sell would need whitelist PDAs for the trader, the vault, the fee account and the integrator in one tail. A containment hook whose only extra account is one fixed per-mint config PDA, holding the allowed sources and destinations, serves every transfer with a two- or three-account tail (hook program, validation account, config), well under the roughly 28 hook accounts that fit the default heap (`pipeline:docs/INTEGRATOR-GUIDE.md:105`). Writability is passed through intact by the router and by the example strategy (`pipeline:programs/pipeline/src/lib.rs:833-840`, `pipeline:programs/pipeline/src/token.rs:148-155`), and Token-2022 de-escalates a writable extra account to read-only if the caller passed it read-only (`phi:src/instructions/execute.rs:262-284`); a read-only config avoids depending on every caller getting that right.
On a buy the hook runs at the last nested level: router (1), strategy (2), Token-2022 (3), hook (4) (`pipeline:docs/ROUTER.md:156-158`). So the containment hook must make no CPI in `Execute`, as the Factory's hook already does ("Makes no outbound CPIs", `fff:README.md:37`).
The Factory is no unlock precedent: its hook has no unlock path, its whitelist is append-only and global to the program, and the transfer-hook authority on its three mints is a 2-of-3 Squads vault with a 24-hour timelock that has not been burned (`fff:README.md:25-26`, `:64-75`; `fff:programs/transfer-hook/src/instructions/add_whitelist_entry.rs`).
Write a new fail-closed containment hook on the Factory's pattern (all accounts required, `transferring` check, no CPI, zero amounts refused), with one read-only per-mint config PDA as its only extra account.
The `pool4.json` strategy (`4ajWF6Y3wsxXUbvvaEu7ZcA2RosB1YfaXcqot8kguwJD`) is not a four-mint pool. It is MetalLegBob's own Pool4 port for Solana: two mints (a Token-2022 token and wSOL), a trim to an inventory cap, a cap ratchet with a daily decay, a SOL buy wall that bought tokens on devnet, burns, keeper tips and a swept fee ledger (partly inferred from on-chain numbers, section 1). Its source is not public, its metadata URL is a placeholder, and it was registered on 2026-11-08.
Measured compute in his port: 15.4k to 16.7k units for a buy, 12.9k to 14.2k for a sell that trims, 28.6k for a sell that fills the wall, against 8.8k to 12.0k for Pipeline's example constant-product strategy.
Program `4ajWF6Y3wsxXUbvvaEu7ZcA2RosB1YfaXcqot8kguwJD`: upgradeable loader, upgrade authority `2yJJsmhvs2SBsKVtiKCG4wBJNoTH67aRNMhSwkupvUHZ` (MetalLegBob's devnet authority, see `research/pipeline-router.md`), last deployed in slot 520151758 (2026-11-08 21:10 UTC), 450,216 bytes (`solana program show`, devnet).
All 8 markets were created by MetalLegBob's bonding-curve strategy (`ETrmUegraeyB7B6fXBfANmV7w9aEwM3wUEUzFHqo5zAb`, crate `bonding-curve`) in its `Graduate` instruction, which calls the Pool4 strategy's `pipeline_seed_liquidity` and then the router's `register_existing_market` (logs of the 8 seeding transactions, devnet).
The wall's SOL can sit in the same wSOL vault as the curve's, so the swap needs no account beyond the output vault; MetalLegBob's port does this (its swaps resolve exactly one strategy account, the output vault).
Exact-out across the boundary is possible but adds an inverse for each segment; MetalLegBob's port is exact-in only, and integrators must honour `supports_exact_out: false`.
Book the removed tokens to a ledger of tokens awaiting burn and split (MetalLegBob's "retired") and the removed SOL to the wall's idle SOL; nothing moves in the swap.
Because the vaults then hold more than the curve's reserves, pass the sum of everything booked against a vault (reserves, fee ledger, retired tokens, wall SOL) as the "recorded reserve" to `credited_input`; passing only the curve's reserve makes `credited_input` return `amount_sent` even when a Token-2022 transfer fee took part of it, and the books drift above the vault (`credited_input` in `crates/pipeline-strategy-interface/src/lib.rs`). MetalLegBob's port checks the same invariant ("The vaults no longer cover the pool's accounts").
MetalLegBob's port instead refuses `RebalanceWall` when the price is too far above a reference; which guard we use is a design choice.
Filling the wall roughly doubles the strategy's cost (about 14k to 16k more in his port), still far under the quoter's default cap of 200,000 units (`docs/STRATEGY-GUIDE.md`, rule 7) and the about 1.3M units left for strategies per transaction.
