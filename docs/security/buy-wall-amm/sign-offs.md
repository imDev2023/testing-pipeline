# Buy wall AMM: the owner's sign-offs

The engineering and security bar accepts a Low finding only with the owner's written sign-off, recorded in the report ([`docs/spec/README.md`, Internal adversarial review](../../spec/README.md#internal-adversarial-review), [`BAR-18`](../../decisions.md#bar-18-hardening-ticket)).
These are the findings of the internal adversarial review of the buy wall AMM ([README.md](README.md)) left open as accepted Lows.
This is not an audit; the findings are described in [round-1.md](round-1.md) and [round-2.md](round-2.md).

## Re-rating

- **H002**, re-rated from Medium to Low by the owner on 2026-11-19 (session of #55), a default MetalLegBob may change: under [`POOL4-49`](../../decisions.md#pool4-49-which-retired-tokens-earn-the-reward-share) the rewards recipient can still draw the creator's published reward budget, `reward_share x (seeded - cap_floor)`, as fast as the cap may fall rather than as demand arrives; round 2 measured it at about 7.8% of a 10 SOL curve's SOL over 4 days at the defaults and up to 21.7% in one day at the most extreme parameters a creator may choose; the spec's explainer risks disclose it ([`docs/spec/pool4-for-solana.md`, Aviary's panel](../../spec/pool4-for-solana.md#aviarys-panel)).
- **R2-02**, re-rated from Medium to Low by the owner on 2026-11-19 (answer 4a on #55), a default MetalLegBob may change: under the hybrid floor a dip held across one slot boundary still lowers the floor, griefing only, with nobody profiting inside the market (row below).

## Accepted Lows

Every row: signed off by the owner on 2026-11-19 (session of #55, answers 3a and 4a recorded on the ticket), a default MetalLegBob may change; R2G-04 and R2G-08 are Info, listed for completeness.

| ID | What remains open | Why it is accepted | What would change it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H002 (residual) | The rewards recipient can wash a buy and its sell back against the decay credit and take the reward share of the cap's fall at once, up to the published budget | A role the creator chose and published, a budget the creator published, the same lifetime take as organic two-way flow, and disclosed in the explainer; the amplifiers (H005, H007, H056, R2-01) are fixed | The one-slot ratchet lag (the ratchet reads the previous slot's holdings, which also closes H040), or a lower `MAX_CAP_DECAY_BPS` |
| H003 (residual) | Any dip since the last placement, even one bought back within its slot, caps the next `place_wall` at that low once (the placement then resets the cap to the price), until idle SOL refills and the wall is placed again; the floor itself stays up | The hybrid floor ([`POOL4-50`](../../decisions.md#pool4-50-when-the-walls-floor-moves), amended 2026-11-19) closes the profitable back-run; what is left moves one placement, takes no SOL out of the market and pays no reward share | A new decision on `POOL4-50`; a placement rule that ignores a dip bought back inside its slot reopens the back-run H003's verification measured, so the two are decided together |
| R2-02 | A dip held across one slot boundary lowers the floor, so a sell as a slot's last swap, then `place_wall` and a buy back in the next slot, relocates the wall for days at the cost of one slot's price exposure (none for a leader holding both slots) | Griefing only, since dip trims burn whole under `POOL4-49`; a price that lasts a slot boundary is what the reference already accepts; holding the floor up for several slots would widen H003's back-run | Bots that buy dips within the slot make it Low by themselves; otherwise a floor that falls only a step per window, decided together with the back-run |
| H008 | The fee recipient can net up to about 0.0008 SOL a loop (sell into the wall, the next slot's first `place_wall`, buy back, `sweep_fees(0)`) at fees of about 1% or more and wall splits of about 20% or less | `POOL4-50` closed the same-transaction form; the loop must win a race with our own keeper each time and is capped by `KEEPER_TIP_CAP` | A change to the tip rule of [`POOL4-15`](../../decisions.md#pool4-15-keeper-jobs-and-tips); either way the spec's "faking work never pays" should state this measured bound |
| H021 | Freezing the program while `DESIGNER_RECIPIENT` is the zero key would lock the designer's 10% of every fee; the treasury key reassigning its own wrapped SOL ATA locks slot 2 until reversed | No third-party path; an operator-ordering precondition of a freeze, which devnet never has | The mainnet launch: a freeze script that refuses while `DESIGNER_RECIPIENT` is zero |
| H024 | The upgrade key can drain every vault, close the program or freeze before `DESIGNER_RECIPIENT` is set; `POOL4-10`'s "nobody has power after the seed" holds only once the program is frozen | The documented, accepted devnet risk; mainnet waits for the router and is frozen before deployment ([`POOL4-18`](../../decisions.md#pool4-18-mainnet-upgrade-policy)) | Any mainnet deployment, which must freeze first; it would be Critical on mainnet with a live key |
| H025 | `seed` takes only amounts, so a seeder who signs without reading the market can commit its capital to a decoy whose recipients are an attacker's | The documented flow creates and seeds in one transaction, launchpads use `pipeline_seed_liquidity`, and no tooling seeds markets it did not create | Tooling that seeds markets others created; the fix is an expected-parameters hash argument to `seed` |
| H026 | A recipient handed over between create and a separate seed transaction | A timing variant of H025, absent from the one-transaction flow | The same as H025 |
| H040 | Anyone can spend a day's decay credit in one transaction with atomic round trips and thin the curve at the full decay rate | No profit for a third party; bounded by the creator's own `cap_decay_bps` and `cap_floor_bps`; sells always complete | The one-slot ratchet lag (with H002's residual), or a shorter `DECAY_CREDIT_WINDOW` |
| H059 | A dust buy before a known large sell spends the hoarded credit and moves the trim one trade earlier | The targeted sell's fill is untouched; the shift is one trade, within one day's allowance; the total reward share is the same, only its timing moves | Decaying the cap at every swap whoever triggers it |
| H068 | The full-tier Kani harnesses (the pricing steps, the wall's close and place, the trim) never run in CI | Z3 proves every one of their statements for every input, Proptest ties the Z3 models to the compiled code in CI, and `proofs/pricing_z3.py` now says so; several full-tier harnesses do not finish in an hour ([maths-properties.md](maths-properties.md)) | A manual or scheduled non-blocking `kani_full` job with a time budget |
| H070 | Anyone can create unlimited CREATED markets naming any seeder, which live forever | No funds at risk; the creator pays the rent; listings show OPEN markets; the gate no longer lets such a market veto a release | A `close_created` instruction, if listing pollution becomes a problem |
| H097 and R2G-06 | A market created between `verify-record` and the upgrade landing (about 10 to 40 seconds of buffer writes, plus start-up) is served by a build never run on it, and after the upgrade that build is its own baseline | Needs an honest creator launching mid-deploy with a market kind absent from the snapshot and the gate's own market; devnet only, recoverable while the upgrade key is held; mainnet is frozen before deployment | Writing the buffer first, then a fresh gate run, then the upgrade at once, with a check of the market list in between |
| H110 | The market opens in the seed, so the seeder chooses the opening price and can trade first | Intended permissionless-launch composability; the price is the seeder's own, and the cap and floor protect later holders | An optional first-swap delay, a new creation parameter |
| H111 | No keeper runner schedule or alerting exists yet | Every job is permissionless, so a stalled runner causes delay, never loss | The keeper runner ticket |
| R2-03 | `place_wall` is refused in every slot that already holds a swap, so continuous trading or a griefer's dust swap per slot starves placement | No value leaves the market and nothing is stranded; the griefer pays per slot; the keeper lands at the top of a slot | Accepting a placement in a traded slot when the price is at or above the floor, placing at the floor |
| R2G-04 | CI trusts fixture baselines with no provenance, so an edited or re-recorded fixture could excuse a fixture-market-specific regression (rated Info by the reviewer) | CI only; layout checks are never excused; the gate's own market catches a uniform regression; cluster runs record a fresh baseline from the deployed program | Refusing fixtures with recorded failures outside a reviewed allowlist, and checking the baseline's build hash against the release records |
| R2G-07 | A gate record covers a market by its key forever, whatever its state becomes after the run | The record is checked with the deployed hash unchanged; devnet only, recoverable while the upgrade key is held | Short-lived records, with the gate run inside `deploy.sh` right before the upgrade |
| R2G-08 | `verify-record` never shows the shared failures a record accepted, and `own_market` is an unsigned field (rated Info by the reviewer) | Records were always unsigned and anyone who can commit can write one, the existing trust boundary | Printing each shared failure before deploying, and a hash of the snapshot and report beside each record |
| H034 | A candidate that moves `SUITE_TREASURY`, raises the share constants or `KEEPER_TIP_CAP`, adds a drain instruction or breaks a handover passes the release gate | The gate guards stranding, not intent ([`POOL4-41`](../../decisions.md#pool4-41-what-the-release-gate-runs-on-each-market)); the upgrade key is trusted and devnet only; the source is public | Asserting in the gate that each payout's recipient balance rose by the owed amount, and printing any step the deployed build failed everywhere that the candidate passes |
| R2G-03 (residual) | The gate's own market takes its due keeper jobs from the candidate's tree, so a tree that refuses a fee slot's wallet is caught only on cluster markets where the deployed build swept that slot, and not on a cluster with no such market | Stated in the spec ([`docs/spec/pool4-for-solana.md`, A release gate](../../spec/pool4-for-solana.md#a-release-gate)); the rest of R2G-03 is fixed, each replay asking the build it runs for a fee slot's wallet | Taking the own market's due jobs from the deployed build too, or from a gate-owned list |
